## Context

SIERA was Evora Global's ESG data platform for real estate investors, tracking energy, carbon and net zero targets.

I joined in 2019 as a UX designer and developer, building its React data-visualisation module. From 2021, as Principal Designer, I led the design direction and set up Pendo and Hotjar to check decisions against real behaviour.

## Analytics that answer a fund manager's questions

<div class="shot-placeholder">Screenshot: fund dashboard or asset passport</div>

- **Fund dashboards:** consumption and carbon, with assets flagged on or off track.
- **Data quality:** coverage and completeness, so estimated figures are visible.
- **Net zero pathways:** carbon intensity against 1.5°C, business-as-usual and target lines.
- **EPC tracking** per asset and portfolio.

<div class="shot-placeholder">Screenshot: net zero pathway chart (fund carbon intensity vs 1.5°C pathway, business-as-usual, target pathway and stranding year)</div>

<div class="callout">

**Lead with "when", not "how much".** Marking the stranding year turns three lines into an answer: *this asset becomes a stranded risk in 2031 unless we act.*

## Rebranding a live product

In 2023 I also led the front-end side of Evora's rebrand in SIERA, moving every hard-coded colour into one source of truth in under three weeks. That work is written up with my other [branding](/projects/branding/) projects.

Working in a large, long-lived codebase taught me small reviewable steps and systems before screens, which shaped the [design system](/projects/design-system/) for the new platform.
